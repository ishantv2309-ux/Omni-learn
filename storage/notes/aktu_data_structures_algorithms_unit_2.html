1. Let $u = x \implies du = dx$ and $dv = e^x dx \implies v = e^x$.

2. Applying the formula: $$\int x e^x \, dx = x e^x - \int e^x \, dx = e^x(x - 1) + C$$

    • Common Mistakes & Exam Pitfalls

  • Omitting the constant of integration $+ C$ in indefinite integrals.
  • Confusing time complexity with auxiliary space complexity under recursive calls.
